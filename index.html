📜 LISTENING TRANSCRIPT

"The 2021 season was one of the most exciting in Formula 1 history. Lewis Hamilton wanted to win his 8th World Championship. He already had 7 titles, and one more would make him the greatest driver ever. But there was a problem: a young Dutch driver named Max Verstappen wanted to win his first title.

Max drove for the Red Bull Racing team. He was only 24 years old, but he was very fast and very brave. Lewis drove for Mercedes. He had more experience, but Max had more energy. The two drivers fought in every race.

They raced in 22 countries that year. Sometimes Lewis won, sometimes Max won. They even crashed into each other at Silverstone and Monza! It was dangerous, but the fans loved it. Everyone watched their battle on TV.

Finally, they arrived at the last race in Abu Dhabi with equal points. 369.5 points each! It was incredible. The winner of this race would become the World Champion. Lewis was very fast. He led the race from the start. Max tried to catch him, but Lewis was too quick.

With only 5 laps to go, everyone thought Lewis was the winner. But then, another car crashed and a safety car came out. All the cars slowed down. Max changed his tyres, but Lewis stayed on old tyres. When the race restarted on the final lap, Max had fresh tyres and Lewis didn't. Max overtook Lewis and won the championship. It was the most dramatic finish ever!"

PHASE 4

Reading: A New Rival

10 MIN
E7.3.R1 Spot specific information about names and dates
F1 WEEKLY 2025

THE 2025 SHOCK: A NEW CHAMPION

Four years later, in 2025, the story was very different. Max Verstappen was the champion for four years in a row. He won in 2021, 2022, 2023, and 2024. Everyone thought he was impossible to beat. But he had a new rival: a British driver named Lando Norris.

Lando was born in Bristol, England in 1999. He started racing when he was only 7 years old. He joined Formula 1 in 2019 and drove for the McLaren team. For many years, his car was not fast enough to win. But in 2025, McLaren built an amazing car.

The season was incredible. Lando won 8 races. Max won 6 races. They fought on every track. Sometimes they touched wheels! The fans loved watching them race. It was like Max versus Lewis again, but with a new rival.

Max drove perfectly all year. He never made a mistake. But the McLaren car was simply faster. Lando was brilliant under pressure. He stayed calm and focused. In the last race in Abu Dhabi, Lando only needed to finish in the top 5 to become champion.

Lando finished second in Abu Dhabi. He beat Max Verstappen by just 2 points! When he crossed the finish line, Lando cried on the radio because he was so happy. It was his first World Title. For Max, it was heartbreak. He lost after four years of winning. But he said, "Lando deserved it. He was the best this year."
