SPORTS MAGAZINE

THE 2025 SEASON: SO CLOSE!

After his big wins, Max Verstappen wanted to win another trophy in 2025. He trained very hard every day to be strong. He moved to Monaco to have a quiet life and focus on racing.

The 2025 season was a big battle. Lando Norris was very fast. Max tried his best to win.

In the final race, everyone watched the points carefully. Max drove a brilliant race, but it was not enough. He lost the championship to Lando Norris. He lost by just 2 points.

Max was sad because he wanted to win. But he congratulated Lando. He promised to come back next year.

Dates Names Vocabulary "to" / "because" Numbers
🔊 Reading Audio: media/audio/reading-text.mp3

📝 Task A: Scanning for Specifics

Question 1: Why did Max move to Monaco?

Target Answer: He moved to Monaco to have a quiet life. (Focus on "Giving explanations/reasons" function)

Question 2: Who won the title in 2025?

Target Answer: Lando Norris.

Question 3: How many points did Max lose by?

Target Answer: He lost by 2 points.

📝 Task B: True or False

1. Max won the championship in 2025.

2. Lando Norris beat Max by 10 points.

(Correction: 2 points)

3. Max moved to Monaco to have a quiet life.

📋 Dialogue Guide

Journalist:

"Hello Max. Can I ask you about your career?"

Max:

"Sure."

Journalist:

"When were you born?"

Max:

"I was born in 1997."

Journalist:

"Tell me about 2021. What happened?"

Max:

"It was amazing. I beat Lewis Hamilton on the last lap."

Journalist:

"And what happened in 2025?"

Max:

"That was sad. I lost to Lando Norris."

Journalist:

"Why did you lose?"

Max:

"Because Lando was very fast. I lost by 2 points."

Assignment / Homework

Take Home

✍️ Task: "Biography of a Legend"

Choose a different sports star or famous person and write a short biography (4-5 sentences).

⚽ Ronaldo
🔬 Marie Curie
🇹🇷 Atatürk
🎾 Serena Williams
🚀 Elon Musk

Your biography MUST include:

  1. When they were born.
  2. Why they moved or worked hard (use 'to' or 'because').
  3. One big success (award) or event.

📝 Example:

"Cristiano Ronaldo was born in 1985 in Portugal. He grew up in a small town called Funchal. He moved to Manchester to play professional football. He won many awards because he trained very hard every day."
