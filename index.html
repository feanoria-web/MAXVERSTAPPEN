"Max Verstappen has a very exciting life story. He was born in Belgium in 1997. He was a brilliant child who loved racing.

He moved to the Formula 1 team when he was very young, in 2015. He wanted to be the best.

His most famous race was in 2021. It was the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix. He was racing against Lewis Hamilton. It was a very difficult race. But on the last lap, Max passed Hamilton. He beat him and won his first big award: The World Championship."

F1 WEEKLY 2025

THE 2025 SEASON: SO CLOSE!

After his big wins, Max Verstappen wanted to win another trophy in 2025. He trained very hard every day to be strong. He moved to Monaco to have a quiet life and focus on racing.

The 2025 season was a big battle. Lando Norris was very fast. Max tried his best to win.

In the final race, everyone watched the points carefully. Max drove a brilliant race, but it was not enough. He lost the championship to Lando Norris. He lost by just 2 points.

Max was sad because he wanted to win. But he congratulated Lando.
