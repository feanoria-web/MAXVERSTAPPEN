📜 LISTENING TRANSCRIPT

"The 2021 season was crazy. Lewis Hamilton wanted to win his 8th title. But Max Verstappen wanted to win his first title.

They raced in many countries. Sometimes Lewis won, sometimes Max won. They even crashed into each other! It was dangerous.

They arrived at the final race in Abu Dhabi with equal points. It was the last chance.

Lewis was very fast in the race. He was leading. Max was behind him. Everyone thought Lewis was the winner.

But then, a safety car came out, and everything changed..."

F1 WEEKLY 2025

THE 2025 SHOCK

Four years later, in 2025, the story was different. Max Verstappen was the champion, but he had a new rival: Lando Norris.

Lando drove for the McLaren team. He was brilliant all year. Max tried to beat him, but the McLaren car was very fast.

The season finished with a very close score. Max drove perfectly, but he did not have enough points.

In the end, Lando Norris beat Max Verstappen.

It was a heartbreak for Max. He lost the World Title by just 2 points. Lando cried because he was so happy.
